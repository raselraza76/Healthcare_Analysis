# 🏥 Healthcare Analytics | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze healthcare billing, treatment costs, insurance coverage, patient expenses, procedures, diagnoses, departments, and service types.

## 📊 Project Overview
![Healthcare Analysis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/raselraza76/Healthcare_Analysis/main/healthcare.png)

This project analyzes healthcare billing data to understand how costs are distributed across different **departments, procedures, diagnoses, and service types**.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of overall healthcare expenses and allows users to filter the analysis by **state, diagnosis, department, service type, and procedure**.

## 🎯 Business Questions

* What is the total healthcare billing amount?
* Which procedures generate the highest billing?
* Which departments contribute the most to total billing?
* Which diagnoses have the highest healthcare costs?
* How much do patients pay out of pocket?
* How much insurance coverage is provided?
* Which service type has the highest billing?
* What is the average medication and treatment cost?

## 💡 Key Insights

From the dashboard:

* Total healthcare billing is approximately **$4.23M**.
* Treatment costs represent a major part of the overall healthcare expenses.
* **Orthopedics** has the highest billing contribution among the departments shown.
* **X-Ray** generates the highest billing among the listed procedures.
* Outpatient services account for the largest billing amount among the service types.
* Insurance coverage and out-of-pocket expenses represent significant portions of healthcare costs.
* The dashboard makes it easier to compare healthcare spending across departments, diagnoses, procedures, and service types.
